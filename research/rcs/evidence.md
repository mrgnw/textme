# RCS for travelers

Evidence snapshot dated 2026-11-26. [Feature matrix](feature-matrix.json) · [Evidence, figures, and primary-source quotes](evidence.json)

## Strongest arguments

- **Start with the phone number you already have.** Rich Communication Services (RCS) can carry receipts, typing, group chat and higher-resolution media across compatible Android and iPhone lines. If the phone's Messages app already handles texts, there is no second messaging account to set up. Both endpoints still need eligible service and compatible clients. [Google Messages help, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/13508703) · [Apple Support, 2026-05-25, vendor-claim](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/122195)
- **One inbox can use more than one transport.** RCS can use Wi-Fi or mobile data when the line is eligible. Short Message Service (SMS) fallback can help when cellular texting still works and internet data is unavailable. Fallback can drop encryption and rich media features, split group threads, or incur fees. [Google Messages help, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/7189714) · [Verizon, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://www.verizon.com/support/verizon-rcs-messaging-faqs/)
- **A verified business has a recognizable identity in the inbox.** Verification and read events can help with travel messages such as tickets and booking notices. Sinch reported a 70% read rate and 5.5% click-through rate for TUI France’s late-2024 travel promotion. Infobip reported 26% higher opens and 66% higher engagement in a revised Telekom promotion. Neither case establishes a general SMS read baseline or a causal channel effect. [Google business messaging help, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/7513219) · [Sinch, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://sinch.com/messaging/customer-stories/tui/) · [Infobip, 2022, vendor-claim](https://www.infobip.com/news/telekom-deutschland-doubles-campaign-performance-rate-with-infobips-rcs-offering)

## Weakest points

- **Encryption depends on the conversation.** Apple and Google began rolling out Android–iPhone end-to-end encryption in beta on 2026-05-11, starting with iOS 26.5 on supported carriers and current Google Messages. The conversation lock is the evidence on a particular thread. iOS 18 RCS lacks this path. WhatsApp, Signal, Messenger personal chats and iMessage already document default end-to-end encryption in their own eligible personal chats. [Apple, 2026-05-11, vendor-claim](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/05/end-to-end-encrypted-rcs-messaging-begins-rolling-out-today-in-beta/) · [WhatsApp, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://faq.whatsapp.com/3398056720476987/) · [Signal, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://signal.org/?lang=en_gb) · [Meta, 2023, vendor-claim](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/12/default-end-to-end-encryption-on-messenger/amp/) · [Apple Support, 2026, vendor-claim](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/104972)
- **Publication and deployment move at different speeds.** GSMA Universal Profile 4.0 specifies interoperable video calls and 4.1 refines encryption. Those releases do not establish widespread calling, compatible recall, or feature parity across Apple and Google clients. Telegram documents file uploads up to 2 GB for free accounts and 4 GB for Premium accounts. A comparable universal RCS attachment ceiling was unverified. [GSMA 4.0, 2026-03-26, vendor-claim](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/article/from-rich-text-to-video-rcs-universal-profile-4-0-has-arrived/) · [GSMA 4.1, 2026-07-28, vendor-claim](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/article/rcs-universal-profile-4-1-stronger-foundations-for-secure-messaging/) · [Telegram, 2022, vendor-claim](https://telegram.org/blog/700-million-and-premium)
- **Verification addresses one part of fraud.** Google documents access to business message content by entities operating the conversation and sometimes carriers. U.S. Federal Trade Commission data show $470 million in reported losses from scams starting with a text in 2024. The data do not identify which message protocol was used. [Google, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://developers.google.com/business-communications/rcs-business-messaging/terms-and-policies/data-security) · [FTC, 2025, measured](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/spotlight-top-text-scams.pdf)

## Traveler caveats

- **Home line roaming.** Verizon documents RCS roaming with an internet connection and warns that some Wi-Fi firewalls can interfere. Data follows the plan. Apple says a home line can still incur roaming fees when the travel eSIM supplies data. [Verizon, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://www.verizon.com/support/verizon-rcs-messaging-faqs/) · [Apple Support, 2026-10-22, vendor-claim](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118227)
- **Local number or data-only eSIM.** A supported local line with its own number can have its own RCS identity. A data-only eSIM supplies internet access without a new RCS number. Apple documents selecting the line for a message. Google’s troubleshooting guide tells dual-SIM users to align the call and data SIMs. Home-line RCS over a secondary line’s internet connection remains unverified across carrier and device combinations. [Apple Support, 2026-11-03, vendor-claim](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/109317) · [Google Messages FAQ, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/9487020) · [Airalo, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://www.airalo.com/help/help/using-managing-esims/how-can-i-receive-sms-and-calls-while-connected-with-my-data-only-esim/9416F6CUOV28)
- **WhatsApp can be the better default for an existing group or a destination where contacts already use it.** An existing account keeps its home number when a local SIM supplies internet access. Re-verification still needs access to that number, and roaming data can still cost money. Ofcom reported 90% UK adult reach in 2025. Brazil’s 2025 ICT Panel found 91% of internet users aged 16 or older used WhatsApp daily. The figures measure different populations and use patterns. [WhatsApp, accessed 2026-11-26, vendor-claim](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5315147411944059/?cms_platform=android) · [Ofcom, 2025-12-10, measured](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/media-use-and-attitudes/online-habits/from-apps-to-ai-search-how-the-uk-goes-online-in-2025) · [Brazilian Internet Steering Committee, 2026, measured](https://www.cetic.br/media/docs/publicacoes/2/en-us/20260408150920/ict_panel_information_integrity_2025_book_en.pdf)

## Data gaps

Public primary sources reviewed here did not establish comparable personal-message delivery rates or latency, bytes per matched text/photo/video, battery use, roaming success across carrier pairs, or spam and phishing incidence by protocol and app. The evidence file records these as null with the nearest source and assessment. GSMA’s weak-coverage claim for Profile 3.1 describes connection design. It supplies no field benchmark. [GSMA, 2025-07-25, vendor-claim](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/article/elevating-the-messaging-experience-with-rcs-universal-profile-3-1/)

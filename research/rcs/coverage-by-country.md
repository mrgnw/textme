## Method and gaps

- **iPhone.** `listed` includes every brand with an RCS or encrypted RCS beta entry in Apple’s directory. Shared network brands can have separate entries. `none_listed` means Apple has a local carrier section with zero RCS entries. `unknown` means the reviewed directories have no distinct section for the market. Apple’s listings can lag carrier rollouts. [Apple setup, May 25, 2026](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/122195) · [United States and Canada, September 15, 2026](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109526) · [Europe, November 17, 2026](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108048) · [Asia-Pacific, November 17, 2026](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109510) · [Latin America and Caribbean, May 11, 2026](https://support.apple.com/en-lamr/109520) · [Middle East and India, February 5, 2026](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/118609) · [Africa, April 1, 2026](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109518)
- **Android.** `live` requires evidence of a broad local consumer rollout or support across multiple major providers. `partial` means the evidence covers a named provider, plan, device family, or other subset. Google’s global rollout had device and provider caveats, and its current help gives line-level checks. Historical launches and business-only coverage can leave current person-to-person service `unknown`. A current country inventory suitable for assigning `none_listed` was unavailable. [Google rollout, November 19, 2020](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/messages/helping-you-connect-around-world-messages/) · [Google Messages availability](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/9487020?hl=en-GB) · [Google Jibe](https://docs.jibemobile.com/intro?hl=en) · [GSMA launch table, July 8, 2020](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/technologies/networks/rcs/global-launches/)
- **Evidence.** `measured` denotes direct extraction from Apple’s inventory or StatCounter’s web measurements. `vendor-claim` denotes reported service or launch evidence, including industry and regulator reports. For an `unknown` without local evidence, the required `estimate` value labels the evidence assessment. It conveys no probability or guess about availability. The prior research records supporting releases for multi-provider claims. [Adoption sources](adoption.md) · [Movistar Android terms, January 1, 2022](https://comunidad.movistar.es/t5/s/movistar/attachments/movistar/ayuda_tecnica_movil/264750/1/cp-rcs.pdf)
- **Platform mix.** Both shares are present for 74 markets and null for 175. Each non-null share preserves the value, date, and source from the existing country dataset. The values describe October 2026 mobile web page views. Contact reach also depends on device and plan eligibility, activation, and interoperability. [Existing country dataset](messaging-by-country.json) · [StatCounter methodology](https://gs.statcounter.com/faq)
- **Traveler use.** A local listing describes eligible local texting lines. For a home line abroad, confirm roaming with the home carrier. A data-only travel eSIM can supply internet while the home line remains the texting line. Check for an RCS label in the conversation to confirm both endpoints can use it. The dataset leaves roaming and individual activation unverified unless a source documents the specific case. [Apple dual-SIM guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317) · [Google conversation checks](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/9592174?hl=en-AU) · [Telcel roaming terms](https://www.telcel.com/personas/servicios/llamadas-y-mensajeria/mensajes-android)
- **Carrier exceptions.** Telstra began an eligible-device rollout on November 16, 2026, after the Australian regulator documented Google-hosted Android coverage. Vodafone Spain excludes its shared-number MultiSIM OneNumber service. Samsung ended Samsung Messages support on U.S. devices running Android 12 or newer.

# Messaging app adoption

Research cutoff November 26, 2026. The [country dataset](messaging-by-country.json) covers 74 markets across the six inhabited continents. It identifies a leading app within the scope of the cited source in 73 markets and gives a usage or reach percentage for that app in 55. The [global dataset](messaging-global.json) records monthly active users or accounts where available, alongside dated estimates and other measures.

## What travelers encounter

- **WhatsApp travels well across Latin America, Africa, and much of Europe.** In the second quarter of 2026, 94.3% of Brazilian, 93.3% of South African, and 86.3% of German internet users aged 16 and older reported using it in the past month. Facebook Messenger led the listed chat apps in the Philippines at 92.8% on the same basis. Viber led in Bulgaria at 83.8% and Greece at 80.6%. [Brazil](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-brazil) · [South Africa](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-south-africa) · [Germany](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-germany) · [Philippines](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-philippines) · [Bulgaria](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-bulgaria) · [Greece](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-greece)
- **Several markets have a clear local default.** In that same survey, LINE reached 78.9% in Japan, 82.5% in Thailand, and 92.0% in Taiwan. WeChat reached 88.8% in mainland China, and Zalo reached 80.8% in Vietnam. In South Korea, 98.1% of surveyed mobile messenger users used KakaoTalk in 2025, while 40.4% used Instagram direct messages. These figures indicate which app a traveler is most likely to encounter. [Japan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-japan) · [Thailand](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-thailand) · [Taiwan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-taiwan) · [China](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-china) · [Vietnam](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-vietnam) · [Nasmedia](https://www.nasmedia.co.kr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025_NPR_%EC%9A%94%EC%95%BD%EB%B3%B4%EA%B3%A0%EC%84%9C_f.pdf)
- **Some markets have several common options.** In the United States, 56.2% of internet users aged 16 and older reported using Facebook Messenger in the past month, 42.2% iMessage, and 33.2% WhatsApp in the second quarter of 2026. The survey ranks the apps it lists. In Australia, 55% of adults used Messenger to send messages in the week before the June 2025 survey, and 96% reported using their phone for text messaging over the previous six months. [United States](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-united-states-of-america) · [Australian Communications and Media Authority](https://www.acma.gov.au/publications/2026-02/report/communications-and-media-australia-how-we-communicate)
- **Access restrictions can change the ranking.** Russia’s MAX led Mediascope’s June 2026 panel with 86.2 million monthly visitors aged 12 and older after network restrictions on Telegram and WhatsApp. Tests in mainland China found WhatsApp, Telegram, and Signal sites blocked on two home broadband networks in November 2026. The Open Observatory of Network Interference documented blocking in Iran and Gabon during specific disruptions. The United Arab Emirates regulates live internet calling, and WhatsApp is absent from its approved calling list. ## How many apps people use

Germany’s regulator estimated 3.10 messaging and video-calling services per user in 2025. South Korea’s Nasmedia survey found a mean of 1.8 messaging services among surveyed messenger users in 2025. Australia’s 2025 mean was 5.5 services over six months, with general social media included. A representative traveler-specific app count was unavailable. [Germany](https://bundesnetzagentur.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/EN/BNetzA/PressSection/ReportsPublications/2025/TK_Annual_Report25_EN.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3#page=48) · [South Korea](https://www.nasmedia.co.kr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025_NPR_%EC%9A%94%EC%95%BD%EB%B3%B4%EA%B3%A0%EC%84%9C_f.pdf) · [Australia](https://www.acma.gov.au/publications/2026-02/report/communications-and-media-australia-how-we-communicate)

## Comparing with Rich Communication Services (RCS)

- **Country reach.** Infobip’s estimates range from 10% of phone users in South Africa to 80% in the United States among the markets tabulated here. The measurement date is undisclosed. The estimate describes business messaging reach. Person-to-person reach depends on both endpoints having supported and enabled RCS. [Infobip country estimates](https://www.infobip.com/blog/most-popular-messaging-apps-by-country) · [Apple messaging support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104972) · [Google and Apple rollout](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/android-ios-end-to-end-encrypted-rcs-messaging/)
- **Mobile operating systems.** StatCounter’s October 2026 Android and iOS shares use mobile web page views as the denominator. Use them to illustrate the mix of platforms a traveler may encounter. Estimating RCS reach requires carrier and device support plus activation data. [StatCounter methodology and CC BY-SA 3.0 terms](https://gs.statcounter.com/faq) · [Apple carrier support by region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109526)
- **Global scale.** Meta reported more than 3 billion WhatsApp monthly active users in April 2025. The GSMA cites a forecast of more than 3.8 billion active RCS users by the end of 2026, with an unspecified activity window. Google’s 2023 disclosure counted over 1 billion monthly active Google Messages users with RCS enabled. Plot the forecast, historical enablement, and app activity with their dates and metric labels. [Meta earnings call](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2025/q1/Transcripts/META-Q1-2025-Earnings-Call-Transcript-1.pdf) · [GSMA forecast](https://www.gsmaservices.com/service-type/network/how-operators-can-leverage-the-billion-dollar-cross-platform-rcs-opportunity-with-gsma-entitlements/) · [Google milestone](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/7-new-messages-features/)

## Sources and data gaps

- **Country rankings.** The main series uses GWI’s second-quarter 2026 self-reported platform use in DataReportal’s Digital 2027 country reports. Local surveys, audience panels, Android rankings, and market reports fill some gaps. Use each row’s ranking scope and confidence in map tooltips. App audiences overlap, and default texting and direct messages inside social apps have uneven coverage. [DataReportal library](https://datareportal.com/reports) · [Similarweb Android rankings](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/research/apps/worldwide-messaging-apps/) · [LINK Mobility rankings](https://www.linkmobility.com/blog/the-most-popular-messaging-apps-across-the-globe) · [Ofcom audience measurement](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/research-and-data/online-research/online-nation/2025/online-nations-report-2025.pdf?v=409837)
- **Global counts.** Current comparable monthly counts were unavailable for iMessage, Instagram direct messages, Viber, Google Messages, and cross-client RCS. Apple reports active devices, Meta reports the whole Instagram platform, and Rakuten reports Viber registrations. Signal’s monthly count is a media estimate with an undisclosed method. The JSON records the original sources, units, dates, qualifiers, and any missing values.

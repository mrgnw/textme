# RCS for everyday messaging across borders

*Research snapshot: November 26, 2026*

Rich Communication Services (RCS) is a practical first choice for everyday messages between eligible Android and iPhone users. It brings group chats, read receipts and richer media to the texting inbox people already use, without requiring both people to join another messaging app. Ask contacts to try it before a trip, and keep a backup for groups and places where it does not work. Both phones and lines must be eligible, and encryption depends on the conversation. [Google Messages](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/13508703) · [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/122195) · [Apple encryption rollout](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/05/end-to-end-encrypted-rcs-messaging-begins-rolling-out-today-in-beta/) (vendor claims).

## Why messaging fragments on a trip

A traveler may use one app for family, another for a local contact and a third for an existing group. The people on each service determine where the conversation happens. Meta reported more than 3 billion monthly active WhatsApp users and more than 1 billion Messenger users in April 2025. Those are vendor claims, not counts of a traveler's reachable contacts. [Meta earnings call](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2025/q1/Transcripts/META-Q1-2025-Earnings-Call-Transcript-1.pdf).

RCS offers another starting point: a phone number and the device's texting app. It can connect compatible Android and iPhone lines in the same conversation. It does not connect directly to a WhatsApp or Instagram group. [Google](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/13508703) · [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/122195) (vendor claims).

## Reach and adoption

Google reported more than 1 billion monthly active Google Messages users with RCS enabled in November 2023. That vendor claim does not establish how many sent an RCS message. A current observed global RCS user count across clients and carriers is unavailable. [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/7-new-messages-features/).

Apple lists RCS on selected carriers in countries including the United States, United Kingdom, Brazil, Japan and India. A listing is not a count of users, and coverage differs by carrier and software. Check your own line and the recipient's line. [Apple North America](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109526) · [Europe](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108048) · [Latin America](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109520) · [Asia-Pacific](https://support.apple.com/en-au/109510) · [India](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/118609) (vendor claims).

## Reliability, efficiency and security

RCS can use Wi-Fi or mobile data. Verizon says roaming RCS requires an internet connection and that some Wi-Fi firewalls can interfere. SMS or MMS fallback may be available when RCS is not; it can lose rich features or encryption, split group threads, and incur fees. Check the transport before sending sensitive information. [Google](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/7189714) · [Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/support/verizon-rcs-messaging-faqs/) (vendor claims).

Comparable delivery rates, latency, data use per matched message, battery use and spam rates are unavailable in the supplied evidence. There is no basis here to claim RCS is faster, uses less data or battery, or receives less spam than the apps. [GSMA's connection design description](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/article/elevating-the-messaging-experience-with-rcs-universal-profile-3-1/) is a vendor claim, not a field benchmark.

Apple and Google began rolling out cross-platform end-to-end encryption in beta on May 11, 2026, starting with eligible iOS 26.5 carrier lines and current Google Messages. Look for the lock in the conversation. Do not assume every RCS chat is encrypted. [Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/05/end-to-end-encrypted-rcs-messaging-begins-rolling-out-today-in-beta/) (vendor claim). Verified business identities can help identify a sender, but do not prove a message is safe. Google says businesses operating a conversation and sometimes carriers can access business-message content. [Google verification](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/7513219) · [Google data security](https://developers.google.com/business-communications/rcs-business-messaging/terms-and-policies/data-security) (vendor claims).

## Traveler scenarios

- **Home line roaming:** Test RCS before leaving and confirm roaming and data charges with your carrier. Verizon documents use while roaming with internet access; conditions differ by provider. [Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/support/verizon-rcs-messaging-faqs/) (vendor claim).
- **Travel data eSIM:** A data-only eSIM supplies internet, not a new RCS number. Whether home-line RCS works over a secondary travel eSIM's connection remains unverified across devices and carriers. Apple warns that the home line may still incur roaming charges. [Airalo](https://www.airalo.com/help/help/using-managing-esims/how-can-i-receive-sms-and-calls-while-connected-with-my-data-only-esim/9416F6CUOV28) · [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118227) (vendor claims).
- **Local line:** A supported local line with its own number can have its own RCS identity. Tell contacts which number to use. Check the selected messaging line; Google advises dual-SIM users troubleshooting RCS to align the call and data SIMs. [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/109317) · [Google](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/9487020) (vendor claims).

## Where WhatsApp still wins

Keep WhatsApp for an existing group until its members agree to move. Its account can retain a home number when a local SIM supplies internet, although re-verification requires access to that number. RCS eligibility varies by carrier and phone, and home-line RCS over a secondary travel eSIM is unverified. WhatsApp documents default end-to-end encryption for eligible personal chats; cross-platform RCS encryption is still rolling out. [WhatsApp roaming](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5315147411944059/?cms_platform=android) · [WhatsApp security](https://faq.whatsapp.com/3398056720476987/) · [Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/05/end-to-end-encrypted-rcs-messaging-begins-rolling-out-today-in-beta/) (vendor claims).

Local habits matter. Ofcom reported 90% UK adult reach for WhatsApp in 2025; Brazil's 2025 ICT Panel found 91% of internet users aged 16 or older used it daily. These measure different populations and behaviors. [Ofcom](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/media-use-and-attitudes/online-habits/from-apps-to-ai-search-how-the-uk-goes-online-in-2025) · [Brazilian Internet Steering Committee](https://www.cetic.br/media/docs/publicacoes/2/en-us/20260408150920/ict_panel_information_integrity_2025_book_en.pdf).

## Before you leave

1. On Android, open Google Messages, tap your profile picture, open Messages settings, then RCS chats, and turn on RCS. Check its connection status. [Google setup](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/13508703) (vendor claim).
2. On iPhone, open Settings, Apps, Messages, then RCS Messaging, and turn it on if your carrier supports it. Update the phone and check your carrier's requirements. [Apple setup](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/122195) (vendor claim).
3. Exchange a test message and photo with each important contact. Check that the conversation says RCS and, where privacy matters, shows the encryption lock. Agree on a backup and confirm roaming, fallback and data charges with your carrier. [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/122195) · [Google](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/13508703) (vendor claims).

## Data gaps and sources

The research does not establish comparable personal-message delivery rates, latency, data consumption, battery use, spam rates or a current global RCS user count. It also does not establish roaming success across carrier pairs or the share of cross-platform conversations encrypted. Country surveys use different populations and time windows; they do not establish RCS reach between two people. The source links above identify each claim; the supporting datasets are [evidence](evidence.json), [feature matrix](feature-matrix.json), [adoption](adoption.json) and [global messaging figures](messaging-global.json) and [country messaging data](messaging-by-country.json).

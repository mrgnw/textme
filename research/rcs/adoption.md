# Global RCS adoption

Research cutoff November 26, 2026. [Chart-ready data](adoption.json)

Rich Communication Services (RCS) is live across both Android and iPhone. Availability for travelers depends on their line, phone, software, and the other participants in the chat. An iPhone needs a supported text-messaging plan. Google Messages on Android can use a carrier service or Google’s service where eligible. Telcel, for example, requires first activation in Mexico and permits subsequent roaming use with internet access. [Apple setup](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/122195) · [Google Messages support](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/13508703?hl=en-gb) · [Telcel roaming terms](https://www.telcel.com/personas/servicios/llamadas-y-mensajeria/mensajes-android)

## Rollout and encryption

Google announced broad US rollout in 2019 and a global Android rollout in 2020, subject to device and provider eligibility. In 2023 it enabled RCS by default for eligible Google Messages users who had left it on or had never opted out. Apple added carrier-dependent personal RCS in September 2024 and business messaging in iOS 18.1. In iOS 26, users can choose a default texting app for SMS, MMS and RCS. The device and rollout arrays give exact dates where published. [Google 2019](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/rcs/upgrading-messaging-android-us-rcs/) · [Google 2020](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/messages/helping-you-connect-around-world-messages/) · [Google 2023](https://support.google.com/messages/thread/229405182?hl=en) · [Apple iOS 18](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/09/ios-18-is-available-today-making-iphone-more-personal-and-capable-than-ever/) · [Apple iOS 18.1](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121161) · [Apple iOS 26 support](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/122195)

GSMA added interoperable end-to-end encryption based on Messaging Layer Security (MLS) in Universal Profile 3.0 in March 2025. Apple and Google began the default-on cross-platform beta on May 11, 2026, for eligible iPhone carrier lines on iOS 26.5 and current Google Messages clients. The lock indicates encryption in that conversation. GSMA published Universal Profile 4.1 in July 2026 with interoperability refinements. Apple’s iOS 27 guide also documents reactions and inline replies over RCS. [GSMA MLS](https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/article/rcs-encryption-a-leap-towards-secure-and-interoperable-messaging/) · [Apple beta](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/05/end-to-end-encrypted-rcs-messaging-begins-rolling-out-today-in-beta/) · [Google beta](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/android-ios-end-to-end-encrypted-rcs-messaging/) · [GSMA 4.1](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/technologies/networks/gsma_resources/rich-communication-service-rcs-july-2026-publications/) · [Apple iOS 27 guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/imessage-overview-iph4e9799206/27/ios/27)

Data gaps

Units differ: WeChat and QQ report accounts, not people; Snapchat and KakaoTalk report quarter averages; “more than” figures are milestone floors. Signal’s figure is a media estimate with an undisclosed method. Apple does not publish iMessage users, Rakuten publishes only Viber registrations, Meta reports Instagram as a whole, and no source counts RCS users across all clients and carriers. The GSMA 3.8 billion RCS figure is a forecast for the end of 2026 with an unspecified activity window.

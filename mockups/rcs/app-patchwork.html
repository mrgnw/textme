How to read the percentages

Unmarked figures: percent of internet users aged 16+ who used the app in the past month (GWI survey, Q2 2026, via DataReportal Digital 2027). Figures marked * use a different base: Australia (adults messaging in the past seven days, 2025), United Kingdom (adults 18+ visiting in the month, 2025), South Korea (surveyed messenger users, 2025), Taiwan (most-used messenger, 2025), Ukraine (Gradus respondents, 2026), Kuwait and Qatar (estimated business-messaging reach, Infobip). Hover or focus a tile for the exact unit.

Data gaps

19 markets lack a share figure, including one without a reliable ranking. Hatched tiles rest on Android-only app rankings or published rankings with unspecified methods. Iran has no reliable current ranking. App audiences overlap, so shares do not add up, and default SMS/RCS texting is excluded from these rankings. ⊘ marks markets with recorded blocks or calling restrictions (dated observations, not a full list).
